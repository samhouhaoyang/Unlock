# Unlock domain glossary

This glossary defines shared product language. [product.md](product.md) owns behaviour.

**Junior software engineer:** Learner making bounded engineering judgments. A support level is not an employee grade or delivery permission.

**Mentor:** Qualified reviewer who explains a decision and independently approves any reusable teaching copy.

**Task:** A real work opportunity with reviewed learning value, delivery risk, target sub-skill, route, and reason. A task is distinct from a fictional case.

**Learning value:** Relevance to this junior's current growth. It varies by learner and sub-skill.

**Delivery risk:** Consequence of an incorrect judgment in real work, distinct from exercise difficulty.

**Route:** One of four ownership choices: junior with support; AI-handled low-risk routine work; senior delivery plus junior synthetic practice copy; senior-owned high-risk work.

**Practice copy:** Fictional, separate exercise derived from a high-risk decision. It cannot affect real delivery or contain customer data.

**Case:** Versioned, reviewed facts, choices, hints, expected evidence, preset patch, and feedback for an exercise.

**Sub-skill:** One assessable judgment component: contract interpretation, discriminating test design, or patch judgment in the first case pack. Progress is tracked separately.

**Attempt:** One learner's case interaction, including first prediction, help/answer exposure, patch decision, time, and outcome.

**Support level:** Maximum help offered by default for a sub-skill, L4 full demonstration through L0 no pre-submission help. A learner can request deeper help during assisted practice.

**Learning stage:** Period at one support level with its own eligible evidence. Any actual level change begins a new stage.

**Independent check:** Fresh case submitted without relevant in-app help or previous answer/patch exposure. This is an in-app eligibility claim, not proof that no external help occurred.

**Exposed case:** A case whose specific hint, draft, answer, or explanatory feedback the learner has seen. Rewording or changing its version does not reset exposure.

**G1 independent check:** Correct fresh regular attempt for a target sub-skill.

**G2 transfer:** Correct fresh no-hint attempt where a decision-relevant condition changes.

**G3 delayed retention:** Correct unseen no-hint attempt at least 72 hours after a stored teaching attempt for that sub-skill.

**Support fading/restoration:** One-step decrease in default help after all current-stage gates pass, or one-step increase after failed transfer/retention. Neither changes delivery ownership.

**Private attempt:** Learner record held locally in P0, not automatically shared.

**Help request:** Learner-previewed selected context deliberately sent for mentor review in a future real workflow.

**Approved teaching copy:** Independently reviewed explanation with cue, principle, applicability, exception, and source. P0 uses pre-reviewed fictional cards; it does not claim real multi-user approval or isolation.

**Preset AI patch:** Authored training material, clearly labelled as synthetic. It is not a live model output.

**Simulated history:** Author-created test/presentation data, never learner evidence. P0 must not use it to pass a gate.
