# Unlock

Unlock preserves opportunities for junior employees to practise workplace judgment, learn from mentors, and demonstrate when they need less assistance. This glossary defines the language shared by the product, its documentation, and its implementation.

## Language

### People and work

**Junior / 新人**:
A person developing a particular workplace judgment skill, regardless of age or seniority in other work.
_Avoid_: Low-value employee, low-intelligence user.

**Mentor / 领域导师**:
A person qualified to explain judgments in the selected domain and review experience intended for others.
_Avoid_: Manager when only the teaching role is meant; elderly expert as an age-based qualification.

**Skill / 技能**:
A specific kind of judgment that a learner can practise and demonstrate independently, such as allocating expenses across service periods.
_Avoid_: Overall employee ability, universal competence score.

**Task / 任务**:
A work opportunity assigned a handling route according to its learning value and delivery risk.
_Avoid_: Case, attempt.

**Case / 案例**:
A defined situation containing the facts and question on which a learner makes a judgment.
_Avoid_: Attempt, task completion.

**Delivery risk / 交付风险**:
The consequence of an incorrect judgment being used in real work.
_Avoid_: Question difficulty.

**Expense allocation judgment / 费用归属判断**:
A judgment about the period and amount of an expense, or whether the available service and performance information is insufficient to decide.
_Avoid_: Journal entry, treating prepayments and accrued liabilities as the same transaction.

### Learning and assistance

**Attempt / 尝试**:
One learner's effort to answer a case, including the assistance received and the judgment submitted.
_Avoid_: Case, evidence of success before submission.

**Practice / 练习**:
An attempt intended for learning in which the learner may obtain help, see a demonstration, or revisit an exposed case.
_Avoid_: Independent check merely because no new hint was requested.

**Independent check / 独立检测**:
An assessment attempt completed without relevant in-app help or a previously exposed answer to that case.
_Avoid_: Proof that no external assistance occurred, assisted practice.

**Support level / 支持级别**:
The deepest assistance normally available to a learner for one skill, ranging from a full demonstration to an independent attempt.
_Avoid_: Employee rank, model intelligence, work permission.

**Support fading / 辅助渐退**:
A reduction in the default assistance available for a skill, justified by its learning evidence.
_Avoid_: Promotion, demotion, proof of expertise.

**Support restoration / 补强支持**:
An increase in available assistance after difficulty or a reasoned mentor intervention.
_Avoid_: Punishment, employee downgrade.

**Learning stage / 学习阶段**:
A period at a particular support level with its own body of learning evidence; an actual level change begins a new stage.
_Avoid_: Support level itself, reusable lifetime evidence pool.

**Exposed case / 已暴露案例**:
A case whose relevant help, answer, draft, or explanatory feedback has already been disclosed to the learner.
_Avoid_: Fresh case merely because it was reopened or its wording was revised.

### Evidence and experience

**Learning evidence / 学习证据**:
A recorded observation of a learner's judgment under known conditions of assistance, case type, and timing.
_Avoid_: Mastery certificate, automatic performance rating.

**Transfer / 情境迁移**:
Independent judgment in a case where facts relevant to the decision differ from the learning example.
_Avoid_: Repeating the same answer, cosmetic variation.

**Retention / 延迟保持**:
Independent judgment after a delay from an identified teaching experience, using an unseen case.
_Avoid_: Immediate retry, time elapsed since the latest page visit.

**Structured rationale recognition / 结构化理由识别**:
Recognition of the relevant evidence and an acceptable explanation among reviewed choices.
_Avoid_: Independently generated explanation, essay assessment.

**Confidence check / 信心检查**:
A comparison of confidence stated before feedback with the correctness of the associated independent judgments.
_Avoid_: Proven calibration, inferred motivation.

**Review concern / 待核查事项**:
A recorded concern that requires a mentor's reasoned resolution before assistance can fade further.
_Avoid_: A failed answer that can be rewritten as correct by acknowledgement.

**Help request / 求助请求**:
A question and selected context that a learner previews and deliberately shares with a mentor.
_Avoid_: Automatic sharing of all private attempts.

**Experience card / 经验卡**:
A reusable explanation of a judgment, its cue, applicability, and exception, with an identified source and explicit review status.
_Avoid_: Raw help-request transcript, automatically authoritative AI output.

**Simulated history / 模拟历史**:
Authored demonstration observations used to show how the learning rules respond to accumulated evidence.
_Avoid_: Real learner results, measured product effectiveness.
