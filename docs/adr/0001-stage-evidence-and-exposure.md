# Preserve stage evidence and case exposure

Accepted in the product-documentation interview. Every actual support-level change starts a new stage, and exposed cases become practice-only on subsequent attempts; prior evidence, exposure, and unresolved concerns remain in history. Reusing past successes or counting answer-informed retries would make the learning claim easier to demonstrate but would obscure whether the learner made a fresh independent judgment, and retroactively repairing such records would lose the original conditions of assessment.

The implementation therefore needs enough provenance to explain each transition and preserve eligibility as it was at submission. See the [accepted decisions](../implementation-decisions.md) for the stage, exposure, and review rules. When fresh evidence is unavailable, the demo remains pending.
