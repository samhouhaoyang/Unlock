# Preserve stage evidence and case exposure

Accepted for the junior SWE v2 product. Each actual L0–L4 support-level change for a sub-skill closes one learning stage and opens a new one with G1–G3 pending. The prior stage's observations remain historical and cannot pass the new stage's gates. Exposed case identities remain exposed across retries, refreshes, stages, and case-version changes.

An eligible independent check has no relevant pre-submission in-app hint, answer, or preset-patch exposure. Eligibility is recorded at submission; later feedback does not rewrite it. A learner can receive general teaching on another case before a fresh transfer. A failed transfer or delayed retention check restores one level of help once, without preserving a stale passed gate. If no fresh case or real 72-hour delayed attempt exists, the gate remains pending.

This boundary prevents answer-informed retries or old successes from appearing as new independent learning evidence. It also means the short presentation can show pending retention honestly. See [accepted decisions](../implementation-decisions.md) and [product.md](../../product.md).
